# Stakeholders (Users of Financial Statements)

## Stakeholders

A stakeholder is anyone who can affect or is affected by the business. Different stakeholders have different information needs.

### Internal Users

| User | Information Needs |
|------|-------------------|
| **Owners (shareholders)** | Return on investment (profitability), safety of investment (liquidity, solvency). Decide to hold or sell shares. |
| **Management** | Detailed operational data (management accounts), not public financial statements. For planning, control, and decision-making. |
| **Employees** | Job stability, remuneration, benefits. |

### External Users

| User | Information Needs |
|------|-------------------|
| **Prospective investors** | Risks and returns, future growth prospects. Need comparable financial information to decide whether to invest. |
| **Lenders (banks, etc.)** | Ability to repay principal and interest (solvency, cash flow). |
| **Suppliers** | Whether the company can pay invoices on time; current liabilities. |
| **Customers** | Continuity of supply; financial stability. |
| **Government/tax authorities** | Taxation, compliance, economic contribution. |
| **Public/media** | Economic contribution, environmental impact, corporate behaviour. |

## Key Distinctions

- **Current owners vs prospective investors**: Both want profitability, but current owners have access to more detailed information and are concerned with dividend decisions; prospective investors rely on published statements and need comparability.
- **Management vs owners**: Management may have different interests (e.g., bonuses vs long-term profit). This is the **agency problem**.
- **Lenders vs suppliers**: Lenders care about total liabilities and equity (long-term solvency); suppliers care more about current liabilities (short-term ability to pay).

## Alternate Questions

1. Why do suppliers care more about current liabilities while long-term creditors care about total liabilities and equity?
2. How do the information needs of a current owner differ from a prospective investor?
3. What is the agency problem between management and owners, and how do financial statements help?
