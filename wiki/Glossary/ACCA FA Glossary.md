# ACCA FA Glossary

## A
- **Accrual basis:** See [[Accrual Basis of Accounting]].
- **Assets:** Resources controlled by the entity as a result of past events, from which future economic benefits are expected.

## C
- **Conceptual Framework:** The IASB's framework for financial reporting, providing the foundation for IFRS standards.

## E
- **Equity:** Residual interest in the assets of the entity after deducting liabilities.
- **Expenses:** Decreases in economic benefits during the accounting period.

## I
- **Income:** Increases in economic benefits during the accounting period, in the form of inflows or enhancements of assets.
- **IAS:** International Accounting Standard (e.g., IAS 1).
- **IFRS:** International Financial Reporting Standard.

## L
- **Liabilities:** Present obligations of the entity arising from past events, the settlement of which is expected to result in an outflow of economic benefits.

## M
- **Materiality:** See [[Materiality]].

## R
- **Revenue:** Income arising in the course of ordinary activities (e.g., sales, fees).
