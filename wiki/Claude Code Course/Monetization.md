# Monetization (Level 10)

Turning everything learned in the course into dollar signs. Principles credited with every 10x/double/triple of Jack's business. ($30k+ deals with *no outbound* — inbound only.)

## The Money Loop (theory of constraints)

Core: **theory of constraints** — find the single biggest constraining factor and fix it; then the whole business speeds up. The constraint is *always one thing* (Lamborghini manufacturer analogy: 100 tires/day, 500 panels/day, but only 1 engine/day → the engine is the constraint, everything else is irrelevant).

Refined question: *"If in three months one thing were true — one thing only — what would make everything else irrelevant?"* (churn at 2%? 100k views/day on Instagram?). Focus there.

## more → better → new

1. **More** — do more of what already works. 3 posts/week → 6; 50 DMs/day → 100. Fastest growth lever. *"Drink an extra coffee. Do more."*
2. **Better** — raise the quality of the same thing (weekly review: better hook, better CTA). Only after you've exhausted *more*.
3. **New** — open a new channel entirely. Only after more AND better are exhausted.

> "A million-dollar business in one business" — don't split into 3-4-5-6 businesses before you're doing ~$80-100k/month in one.

## Business first, AI second

The biggest money mistake: going to AI and throwing stuff at the business. "It is so easy to become a busy idiot." **Find the constraint → apply AI as a high-impact missile pointed at it** → measure → get a larger problem → repeat. Never-ending cycle.

## Deconstructing what works (content)

- **Deconstruction**: people vote with their views. Look at what's *already working*, extract the pattern, make one change.
- **IG outliers system** built with **Apify** (scraper) + Claude: refreshes weekly, surfaces outlier content + hooks from the AI niche; editor picks 7 reels → then script → publish. Half the strategy = **repurposing YouTube intros into shorts.**
- **Volume matters for signal**: with 7 reels varying from 20k to 900k views, ask "what do the 20k and 900k have in common? do more of that."
- Mr. Beast frame: *"I just need to keep doing content that makes 100 million views. Everything else is downstream."* Low-effort/news content gets ruthlessly commoditized by AI — the differentiator is quality + scale.

## Attention is everything downstream of media

- **Build a personal brand** (YouTube preferred — a trust platform). Everything sits downstream of media.
- **Value × eyeballs**: be as valuable as possible to as many people as possible.
- Inbound > outbound: *"The best clients are inbound; you earn them by being publicly useful first."* Outbound still works but needs a differentiator. Content pre-qualifies: featured in the Times ⇒ "must be something cool."
- "Where attention goes, money flows." Harder initially, easier long term.

## The $10k deal ladder: everything is a diagnostic

Fastest route from zero → $10k: **audit → build → retain**, using the auto-shop "diagnostic" tactic — *you get paid to pitch your product.*

1. **Call part 1**: why we're on the phone (their problem — e.g. "not enough clients on Instagram").
2. **Cost of inaction**: quantify — "you make $5k/client; not getting 10 clients/month = losing $25k/month." Never work with a business you won't create ROI for.
3. **Sell the audit** (the 20%→80% packed at $1k-3k, delivered in 5 days).
4. **Post-call questionnaire** detailing all problems/specifics.
5. **On the findings call**, run through opportunities + constraints, then present **two options: maintenance vs growth**. The small investment makes the larger ($10k-20k) investment far more likely.

**Gateway product = websites.** Give them away for free → blow them away → build the relationship → sell retainers. "If you only sell one thing, sell websites: high impact, visible, easy to build, everyone knows they need one, and Claude Code makes you fast; you can't compete with $0." Use the audit first to find the bottleneck (if it's the website, perfect).

## Productize & retain

- **Productization**: when you keep delivering the same thing to the same avatar, turn it into a product (e.g. an IG-outliers tool sold at $1k + $25/mo).
- Retention: weekly > monthly > quarterly > annual — shorter billing cycles stick; aim for high LTV.
- Scale new channels one at a time, ruthlessly.
