# IAS 1 – Presentation of Financial Statements

**Summary:** Sets out the overall requirements for the presentation of financial statements, including guidelines for structure and minimum content.

## Scope
- Applies to all general-purpose financial statements.
- Also applies to consolidated financial statements.

## Key Requirements
- **Components:** statement of financial position, statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income, statement of changes in equity, statement of cash flows, and notes.
- **Fair presentation:** financial statements must present fairly the entity's financial position, performance, and cash flows.
- **Going concern:** management must assess going concern ability.
- **Accrual basis:** except for cash flow information, financial statements are prepared on the accrual basis.
- **Materiality and aggregation:** separate presentation of material items.
