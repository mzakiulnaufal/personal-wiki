# IFRS 16 – Leases

**Summary:** Sets out the principles for recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of leases. Replaces IAS 17. Lessees recognize a right-of-use asset and a lease liability for most leases.

## Scope

Applies to leases of all assets (except mineral rights, biological assets, etc.). Lessors continue to classify leases as operating or finance.

## Lessee Accounting

- **Recognition:** Recognize a **right-of-use asset** and a **lease liability** at commencement date.
- **Initial measurement:** Liability = present value of lease payments; Asset = liability + initial direct costs + prepayments.
- **Subsequent measurement:** Liability – amortized cost; Asset – depreciated (straight-line) + impairment.
- **Exemptions:** Short-term leases (≤12 months) and low-value assets (e.g., laptops) can be expensed straight-line.

## Lessor Accounting

- **Finance lease:** transfers substantially all risks/rewards; lessor recognizes a receivable.
- **Operating lease:** does not transfer substantially all risks/rewards; lessor retains asset and recognizes lease income straight-line.

## Relevance to ACCA FA

- Replaces IAS 17 for lessees; lessor accounting similar.
- FA syllabus expects understanding of basic lease classification and impact on SOFP.
