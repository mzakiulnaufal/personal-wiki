# IFRS S1 – General Requirements for Sustainability-related Financial Disclosures

**Summary:** Requires entities to disclose material information about sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could affect their cash flows, access to finance, or cost of capital in the short, medium, or long term.

## Key Requirements

- **Objective:** Provide useful information to primary users for decision-making.
- **Core content:** Governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics & targets.
- **Materiality:** Based on the same concept as in IFRS accounting standards.
- **Location:** Can be in the annual report or a separate sustainability report.
- **Comparability:** Requires comparative information for prior periods.
