# IFRS S2 – Climate-related Disclosures

**Summary:** Requires entities to disclose information about climate-related risks and opportunities that could affect their financial position, performance, and cash flows.

## Key Requirements

- **Governance:** Disclose governance processes for climate-related risks.
- **Strategy:** Describe the impact of climate-related risks and opportunities on the entity's business model and strategy.
- **Risk management:** How climate risks are identified, assessed, and managed.
- **Metrics and targets:** Greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3), climate-related financial impacts, and progress against targets.

## Scope

- Applies to all entities (proportionality applied for SMEs).
- Must disclose both financial and non-financial climate-related information.

## Relevance to ACCA FA

- Mentioned in the syllabus under non-financial reports.
- Companies increasingly include climate-related disclosures in their annual reports.
- Understanding the basics of climate-related financial disclosure is relevant.
