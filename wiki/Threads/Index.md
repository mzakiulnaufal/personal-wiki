## Key Themes

- **Content workflow:** Research audience pain points → generate with AI → post consistently → analyze results → feed back into AI.
- **Personal story as fuel:** Use personal experiences (KKN reports, family stories, work anecdotes) as raw material, enhanced by AI.
- **Structure matters:** Hook from a relatable POV, body with actionable steps, and CTA that encourages sharing (not just follow).
- **AI agnosticism:** Use multiple AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, etc.) for research and writing; each has strengths.
- **Monetization simple:** Place Shopee Affiliate links casually in the last thread; even if people don't buy the linked product, the cookie can earn commission on other purchases.
- **Resilience:** Even if your account gets banned, having a system and data allows you to rebuild quickly.
