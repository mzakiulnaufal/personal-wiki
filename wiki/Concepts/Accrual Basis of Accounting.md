# Accrual Basis of Accounting

**Summary:** Recognizes transactions when they occur, not when cash changes hands. Income is recorded when earned, expenses when incurred, regardless of payment timing.

## Key Principles
- **Revenue recognition:** Revenue is recognized when the performance obligation is satisfied.
- **Expense recognition:** Expenses are matched to the period they help generate revenue (matching principle).

## Why It Matters
- Produces a more accurate picture of financial performance than cash basis.
- Required under IFRS and most GAAP frameworks.
- Underpins the statement of profit or loss and statement of financial position.
