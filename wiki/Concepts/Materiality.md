# Materiality

**Summary:** Information is material if omitting or misstating it could influence the economic decisions of users. It is a threshold concept: not all items need separate disclosure if they are insignificant.

## Application
- Materiality is applied when determining what to include in financial statements.
- It is both quantitative (amount) and qualitative (nature).
- Professional judgment is required.

## Examples
- A misstatement of 5% of profit may be material.
- A small amount related to a related-party transaction may be material due to its nature.
