Tips buat bikin output deep research lo 10x lebih baik:  
  
Pecah riset lo jadi 5-6 langkah penelitian yang lebih kecil  
  
Jalanin masing-masing secara terpisah  
  
Baru minta LLM-nya gabungin semuanya  
  
Hasilnya? 10x lebih dalam daripada satu prompt panjang yang lo lempar sekaligus​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​  
  
Gue kasih contoh konkretnya  
  
Topik: Pain points of parents with toddlers  
  
Daripada lo prompt satu kali kayak gini:  
“Riset target audience gue: parents with toddler, cari tau pain point mereka”  
  
❌ Hasilnya? Generik. Dangkal. Ga bisa dipake.  
  
Pecah jadi 6 research session terpisah:  
  
Step 1 → Physical pain points  
“Research the top physical and daily routine struggles parents face when raising toddlers aged 1-3. Focus on sleep deprivation, feeding difficulties, and physical exhaustion.”  
  
Step 2 → Emotional & mental pain points  
“Research the emotional and psychological burdens of parenting toddlers. Include mom guilt, identity loss, anxiety, and relationship strain with partner.”  
  
Step 3 → Financial pain points  
“Research financial pressures specific to parents with toddlers in Indonesia. Include daycare costs, formula, healthcare, and opportunity cost of career breaks.”  
  
Step 4 → Social & community pain points  
“Research the social isolation and support system gaps experienced by young parents with toddlers, especially in urban Indonesia.”  
  
Step 5 → Information overload pain points  
“Research how parents with toddlers are overwhelmed by conflicting parenting advice from doctors, social media, family, and AI tools.”  
  
Step 6 → Aspirations & hidden desires  
“Research what parents with toddlers secretly wish for — not just problems, but dreams, identity, and what they want to become.”  
  
Setelah 6 step selesai, baru lo prompt terakhir:  
  
“Merge all research above into a comprehensive target audience profile. Identify the top 3 deepest pain points, their emotional triggers, and the exact language they use to describe their struggles.”  
  
Hasilnya beda jauh bro. Bukan cuma “orang tua kelelahan dan butuh support.”  
  
Tapi lo dapet kalimat-kalimat yang persis dipakai sama mereka yang bisa lo jadiin hook, copy, dan positioning produk lo langsung.  
  
Context beats content. Always.
